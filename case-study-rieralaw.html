Case study

Riera Law Firm

How a securities arbitration practice went from infrastructure chaos to a bilingual, 70-page website that earns trust.

The challenge.

Jorge Riera is a former SEC enforcement attorney running a nationwide securities arbitration practice from Coral Gables, Florida. When Sebby got involved, the firm's digital presence was in disarray.

  • Two competing websites lived on the same domain, causing DNS conflicts that confused visitors and search engines
  • The firm's professional email had stopped delivering reliably — messages from clients and courts were going missing
  • Plugin licensing conflicts were creating security vulnerabilities on a site handling sensitive client inquiries
  • The existing site didn't reflect the caliber of a former federal prosecutor's practice
  • No Spanish-language presence despite serving a bilingual client base across the country
rieralaw.com
Visit the live siterieralaw.com →

The approach.

Complete rebuild

Built the entire site from scratch in WordPress and Elementor — 70+ pages covering every practice area with detailed legal content approved by Jorge.

Full bilingual site

Every page in English and Spanish with proper hreflang implementation, so Google serves the right language to the right visitor.

DNS & email restoration

Untangled the dual-site DNS conflict, restored clean routing, and fixed email delivery with proper SPF and DKIM configuration.

Plugin audit & security

Resolved all plugin licensing and compatibility issues without a single minute of downtime for a live legal practice.

Custom design system

Created a cohesive visual identity with typography, color palette, and reusable layout components that reflect the authority of a federal-level practice.

Ongoing website care

Set up a monthly Website Care plan covering security updates, daily backups, content changes, and performance monitoring — so the site stays as good as the day it launched.
