Case study

Elite Care Recovery

How a family-owned orthopedic recovery company got a professional website that showcases their equipment and makes it simple for patients to reserve online.

The challenge.

Elite Care Recovery is a family-owned orthopedic recovery business in South Florida, founded by two brothers with 23 combined years in the field. They rent premium NICE1 cold and compression therapy equipment to post-operative patients, with white-glove delivery straight to the patient's door. But without a professional website, they were invisible to the surgeons and patients who needed them most.

  • No website at all, relying entirely on word-of-mouth referrals in a competitive South Florida healthcare market
  • No way for patients or surgeons to see the NICE1 system's features, specifications, or benefits before renting
  • Patients had no online path to reserve equipment, so every booking required a phone call during business hours
  • Competing against established medical equipment companies with polished digital presences and bigger marketing budgets
  • Their white-glove service model and patient-first approach had no digital showcase to differentiate them

The approach.

Custom build from scratch

Built a clean, professional healthcare website with zero templates. Static HTML hosted on GitHub Pages for maximum speed and reliability, with no WordPress overhead, no plugins to update, and no hosting fees.

NICE1 product showcase

Created a dedicated product section with specifications, temperature ranges, compression features, and benefits. Patients and referring surgeons can see exactly what the equipment does before making a decision.

Online reservation form

Built a reservation form directly into the site so patients can request equipment from their phone or computer at any hour, reducing friction in the recovery process and capturing leads 24/7.

Mobile-first design

Designed every page for the way patients actually use it: from a hospital bed, on their phone, often right after surgery. Every button, form field, and text block is optimized for touch and readability on small screens.

Cohesive brand identity

Developed a visual identity that communicates professionalism and care. From the color palette to the typography, every design choice helps Elite Care stand apart from generic medical equipment providers.

Speed and reliability

Hosted on GitHub Pages for sub-second load times and near-perfect uptime. No server maintenance, no security patches, no monthly hosting bill. The site loads fast on any connection, which matters when a patient is researching recovery options from a hospital.

Why this matters for healthcare businesses.

In healthcare, trust is everything. Patients are making decisions about their recovery while they are in pain, often under time pressure. A professional website is not a luxury. It is the first place a patient or surgeon looks to decide whether your business is credible. Here is what a strong healthcare website needs to do:

Load instantly on hospital Wi-Fi

Patients are often browsing on slow hospital networks from their phones. A site that takes five seconds to load loses them. GitHub Pages delivers content from a global CDN for sub-second performance everywhere.

Work perfectly on any phone

More than 70% of healthcare site visits come from mobile devices. If your reservation form is hard to fill out on a phone, patients will call your competitor instead.

Look as professional as you are

Surgeons refer patients to businesses they trust. A polished website signals that you take your business seriously. A missing or outdated site signals the opposite.

Make it easy to take action

A clear reservation form, a visible phone number, and business hours right on the page. Patients should never have to hunt for how to reach you.
