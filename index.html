Common questions.

Straight answers before you book anything.

Still wondering? Ask me on a free call →
How much does a website cost?

Every project is different, but most small business websites fall between $2,500 and $9,000 USD. I'll give you an exact number after our first conversation, no surprises, no hourly billing that creeps up.

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on size. A small site runs 2–3 weeks from kickoff to launch, a custom one 4–6, and the largest builds 6–10. You'll see the design within the first week and get updates throughout. I won't disappear for a month and come back with something you didn't ask for.

Do I own my website?

Yes, 100%. Your domain, your hosting account, your content, your code, it's all yours. If you ever want to part ways, I hand everything over cleanly. No hostage situations.

What if I already have a website?

I can redesign it, rebuild it on better technology, or just take over its care. We'll talk about what's working, what isn't, and whether a refresh or a full rebuild makes more sense for your business.

Do you work with businesses outside the US?

Yes. I work with clients across the US and anywhere English or Spanish is spoken. Everything is done remotely, and timezone differences have never been a problem.

Are you related to the Sebby clothing brand?

No. Made by Sebby is a web design studio in Miami, run by Sebastian Podgaetz. There is a separate apparel company called Sebby Collection that sells women's jackets and coats. We are not affiliated with them, we do not sell clothing, and neither company has anything to do with the other. If you are looking for a jacket, you want them. If you want a website, you are in the right place.
