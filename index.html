Common questions.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is different, but most small business websites fall between $2,000 and $8,000. I'll give you an exact number after our first conversation — no surprises, no hourly billing that creeps up.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most projects take 3 to 6 weeks from kickoff to launch. You'll see the design within the first week and get updates throughout. I won't disappear for a month and come back with something you didn't ask for.

Do I own my website?

Yes, 100%. Your domain, your hosting account, your content, your code — it's all yours. If you ever want to part ways, I hand everything over cleanly. No hostage situations.

What if I already have a website?

I can redesign it, rebuild it on better technology, or just take over its care. We'll talk about what's working, what isn't, and whether a refresh or a full rebuild makes more sense for your business.

Do you work with businesses outside the US?

Yes. I'm based in the Dominican Republic and work with clients in the US, Latin America, and anywhere English or Spanish is spoken. Everything is done remotely — timezone differences have never been a problem.
