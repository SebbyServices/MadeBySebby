What counts as one edit

One edit is up to 30 minutes of work on content you provide. Swapping a photo, updating text, fixing a link, adding a testimonial, changing hours or prices. If a request runs past 30 minutes I tell you before I start, and the extra time is $125/hour, only with your approval.

Not included: new pages, redesigns, new features, writing copy from scratch, or anything needing design direction. Those are quoted separately, and I will tell you honestly when a request has crossed that line.

Packs expire 12 months from purchase and unused edits are not refundable. Edits are used one at a time, in any order, whenever you need them.

Edit packs work on sites I built and on sites I did not. If yours is one I have not seen, the first edit includes a quick look to confirm I can work in it safely.

Fair questions.

What if you didn't build my website?

That's fine. Every plan starts with a free health check so we both know what shape your site is in. If the health check finds preexisting problems, I'll quote that remediation separately and the plan starts once your site is on solid footing. Most sites I care for were built by someone else.

What counts as included edit time?

Changes to existing pages using content you provide: swapping photos, updating hours or prices, adding a testimonial, tweaking text. Included time does not roll over month to month. Work beyond your included hours is $125/hr, always approved by you first. New pages, redesigns, copywriting, online stores, and custom features are quoted separately.

What happens if my site goes down or gets hacked?

I usually know before you do. Monitoring alerts me the moment something's wrong, and getting you back online becomes my first priority. Standard and Growth plans include restoration from a verified clean backup. If the issue requires deeper malware cleanup, forensics, or third-party remediation, I'll scope and quote that work before any additional billing.

Can I cancel?

Monthly plans: Cancel any time, no penalty. Your site, your backups, and your accounts are yours. I'll hand everything over cleanly if you ever move on.

Annual plans: You have 14 days after payment (or renewal) to cancel for a full refund. After 14 days, the annual payment is non-refundable. You'll receive a reminder 30 days before each renewal so you're never surprised.

Do edit hours roll over?

No, included hours do not roll over month to month. Any work beyond your included time is billed at $125/hr, always with your approval before I start.

How does annual plan renewal work?

Annual plans auto-renew each year. I'll send you a reminder 30 days before your renewal date. If you want to cancel, just let me know before the renewal and you won't be charged. If the renewal goes through and you change your mind, you have 14 days for a full refund. No fine print, no runaround.

What if I need something bigger?

New landing pages, redesigns, or custom features go beyond regular care. I'll scope it separately and give you a clear quote before any work starts. Care plan clients get priority scheduling and preferred rates.

What about e-commerce or complex sites?

These plans cover standard business and brochure sites. WooCommerce, online stores, membership sites, LMS platforms, multisite, and heavily custom builds need more hands-on time and are priced individually after the free health check.
