My site exists but it is old. Repair or replace?Mi sitio existe pero está viejo. ¿Reparar o reemplazar?

Depends what is wrong with it. Slow, ugly and out of date is often repairable and much cheaper than starting over. Built on something abandoned, or dependent on a plugin that no longer has an author, usually is not, because you would be paying me to maintain a countdown. I look before I quote, and I have talked people out of a rebuild more than once.Depende de qué le pase. Lento, feo y desactualizado suele tener arreglo y sale mucho más barato que empezar de cero. What moves the price up or down?¿Qué hace que el precio suba o baje?

Three things, in this order. How many pages, because each one is a piece of writing and a layout. Whether you have usable photographs, since stock imagery of somebody else's boat is visible from a mile away. And whether the site has to do something beyond informing, like taking a booking or a deposit. Everything is listed on the pricing page, including what costs extra, so there is nothing to discover on the invoice.Tres cosas, en este orden. Cuántas páginas, porque cada una es un texto y un diseño. Si tienes fotos aprovechables, porque una imagen de banco del barco de otro se nota a kilómetros.
